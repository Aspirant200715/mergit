# Mergit logo brief

The exploration files and generators were removed after the final draft; this kit is the record.

## What Mergit is

Mergit lets you hire AI agents and pay only for work that is proven done: the agent's
work is recorded as a signed receipt, the payment is held until an audit of that receipt
passes, and settlement happens on an Ethereum L2. Motto: "pay for what you got."

## The one idea the mark carries

**Held in trust until proven.** Work and payment are bound together, and the value
in the middle is released only when the work checks out.

## Goals (every option is judged against these)

1. **Ownable.** Reads as Mergit, not as Git, Ethereum, Chainlink or any shape on the
   avoid list below.
2. **Says trust and on-chain without a cliché.** The idea of binding, holding and
   settling, shown through form rather than a padlock, shield-and-tick or token symbol.
3. **Works small.** Clear at 16 px in a browser tab, in one colour, on light and dark.
4. **Belongs to the product.** Same drawing logic as the dashboard's icons and the same
   family as the Onest wordmark (see "Design system").
5. **Scales into a system.** One symbol that works as favicon, nav mark, GitHub avatar,
   app icon and social image, plus a wordmark and lockups.

## Personality

Trustworthy, precise, calm, technical, open. Closer to Linear and Vercel than to a
crypto token: no glow, no hype.

## Audience

Developers and engineering teams who run AI agents, and people who care about on-chain
accountability. Sceptical of hype; have seen every AI and crypto logo.

## Where it lives

Browser tab (16-32 px), dashboard nav (32 px badge next to "Mergit" at 17 px), GitHub
org avatar (circle crop), README header, docs, social share image (1200 x 630), slides.

## Design system (from the Porcelain dashboard, PR #4)

- Colour: ink `#0E1018` on porcelain `#F4F5F7`; dark mode inverts to `#ECEEF2` on
  `#0B0C0F`. Mint `#0E9F74` (dark `#3CCB98`) means verified; azure `#1F6BFF` means action.
- Type: Onest; the nav brand is Onest SemiBold (600), 17 px, letter-spacing -0.01em.
  DM Mono for data.
- Icons: Lucide style, 24 grid, 2.4 stroke (10% of the grid), round caps and joins.
- Shapes: fully rounded pills and circles (radius 999px / 50%), cards at 12-24 px.
- Today's nav mark: a stock Lucide git-merge glyph in an ink circle (to be replaced).

## What the user has liked so far

- Woven or interlaced bands that hold a centre: references `02-woven-m-blue.png`,
  `03-woven-m-eth-gem.png` (an M made of bands around a diamond) and
  `04-shield-woven-diamond.jpg` (crossing ribbons over nested diamond rings).
- A held centre: round 4 D6 (diamond inside brackets, asked for four sides); round 5 G2;
  round 6 H1 (two stems, a broken diamond of bars, a gem ring).
- The mint-green colour of references 04 and 05; the shield silhouette of 01, 04, 05.
- Earlier shortlist: two branches into one block, ether gem, linked blocks, honeycomb,
  proof ring.
- Disliked: anything that looks generic, stock or "not professional"; drawn designs that
  ignore the references.

## Avoid (owned shapes and clichés)

Lone hexagon or isometric box (Chainlink, Arbitrum); a diamond on its own or the ETH
gem (reads as the Ether token); the git branch-and-merge node icon (Git and GitHub);
solid triangle (Vercel); square with one diagonal (Stripe); circle with a square cut out
(Coinbase); soft solid square (Base); lightning bolt; starbursts and sparkles; robot
heads, brains, neural meshes; padlocks; shield with a tick; gradients, glow and 3D in
the master artwork.

## Decisions (confirmed 2026-10-24)

1. **Shield: no.** Show trust through form (binding, holding, settling), not a shield.
2. **Colour: explore widely.** Concepts are drawn in one colour first; once there is a
   shortlist, try many treatments (ink only, mint accent on the held part, full mint,
   azure, two-tone, gradient for marketing only) rather than fixing one up front.
3. **Wordmark: "Mergit"**, sentence case, matching the dashboard.
4. **Be creative.** Do not narrow early: each lens explores freely within the goals.

## Phase 1 lenses (one parallel designer each)

1. **Woven M**: interlaced bands that form an M, over-and-under, flat (refs 02, 03).
2. **Held centre**: two interlocking forms holding one solid centre between them, escrow
   (D6, G2, H1, ref 04).
3. **Wordmark-led**: "Mergit" in Onest with one signature detail; the symbol is taken
   from that detail, the way Stripe's came from its i-dot.
4. **Receipt chain**: interlocking links where the last one is solid; work, verified,
   settled.
5. **Attempt to outcome**: a path that resolves into one solid form; pay for the outcome,
   not the attempt.
6. **Negative space**: the held value exists only as the space between two forms, or a
   letter hidden in the gap (the FedEx-arrow kind of idea).
