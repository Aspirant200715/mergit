# Originality log

## 2026-10-24: chosen direction (trail-to-M with a mint gem in the valley, clasp-g wordmark)

Text searches (web):
- "M lettermark logo with diamond gem in the middle crypto company"
- "web3 crypto company logo letter M with diamond"
- "Mergit logo / Mergit company brand"

Findings:
- No company found using an M whose valley holds a gem, or an M built from a dotted trail.
- Generic stock templates exist of "a letter M inside a diamond shape" (Adobe Stock 1102848832,
  design.com "tech crypto letter" template, many Dribbble concept shots tagged blockchain).
  These put the M inside a diamond outline; the Mergit mark puts a small gem inside the M's
  valley and adds the trail, so the construction differs, but "M + diamond" is a crowded
  stock theme: keep the gem small and the trail visible so the mark stays distinct.
- No existing brand named Mergit found. Nearest name: "Merit" (a Pennsylvania marketing firm),
  a different industry.
- Owned-shape check against `reference.md`: not a lone diamond (ETH), not a hexagon (Chainlink),
  not the git merge icon. The gem is a brilliant cut (flat top, point down), not the ETH
  octahedron. Route C noted a faceted gem sits near Sketch's logo; the combined mark uses the
  gem only as a small held element inside the M.
